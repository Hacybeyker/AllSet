AllSet is a catalog of hands-on examples for developers. Each demo solves a real case with Jetpack Compose and the current platform APIs, so you can try it on your phone and see how it behaves. The app's interface is in Spanish.

What's inside:

• Keyboard and forms: keyboard by data type, action to move to the next field, autofill and fields the keyboard never covers.
• Bottom Sheet: modal and standard Material 3 bottom sheets.
• Animations: shared elements, content animations and screen transitions that follow the predictive back gesture.
• Pager: onboarding with HorizontalPager and Material 3 carousels.
• Lists: sticky headers, swipe to mark or delete with undo, pull to refresh and horizontal rows.
• WebView: pages bundled with the app, messages between the page and the app, and external links in Custom Tabs.
• Notifications: Android 13 permission, channels, big text, picture and progress styles, actions and opening the app from a notification.
• Settings: light, dark or system theme and dynamic color, saved with DataStore.
• Image effects: Ken Burns effect and gesture zoom.
• Tabs: fixed and scrollable tabs with badges.
• Clipboard: copy and paste, sensitive content and reading only when you tap "Pegar" (Paste).
• Spotlight tutorial: highlights elements of the screen step by step.

Privacy: AllSet does not collect or share data, has no ads or analytics and does not connect to the internet.
